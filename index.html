How to use TF Photo Studio

1 · Crop & size

Pick a size (or Custom in mm, cm, inch or pixels). Drag the box over the face; corners resize it. Use 4-Point fix to straighten a photo taken at an angle — drag the 4 dots onto the corners.

2 · Background

Auto remove background works best with a plain wall behind the person. Tap leftovers with the magic wand or paint with the brush. Undo with Ctrl + Z. Then pick a backdrop color.

3 · Print layout

Choose paper (4R, 5R, A4, Letter) and how many passport and stamp photos you need. The app arranges them automatically and tells you if something doesn't fit.

4 · Export & print

Download the sheet as JPG or PDF, or print directly. In the print dialog select the same paper and 100% / Actual size. For online forms, use "For online applications" to set pixels and a KB limit.

Resize & Compress

Add one or many images, choose a size and a maximum KB (e.g. 100 KB). The app finds the highest quality that stays under the limit. 1 KB is treated as 1000 bytes so results pass every website's check.

Private & offline

Nothing is uploaded — all processing happens in your browser. Save this HTML file to your PC or phone and it works without internet.

Reset everything?

All crop, background, retouch, color and layout changes will be undone and the photo returns to how it was when you opened it.
